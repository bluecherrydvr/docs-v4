---
title: "Recordings"
description: "Find, play, and download your camera recordings."
---

Open **Recordings** in the sidebar to browse your stored video.

The list shows the newest recordings first. Each row shows **Date & Time**, **Device**, **Duration**, and **Size**.

A recording that is still being written shows **In Progress** as its duration.

## Find a recording

Use the **Filter by Camera** dropdown to show one camera. Pick **All Cameras** to show every camera again.

Changing the filter returns you to page one.

Use **Previous** and **Next** to move through the pages. The header shows **Page X of Y** so you know where you are. Each page holds 20 recordings.

If nothing matches, the list shows **No recordings found.**

## Play a recording

Click the **Play** button on a row to open the player.

The player opens in a dialog. Its title names the camera and the recording start time. The video starts playing with standard playback controls, and you can seek within indexed formats.

If playback fails, you see an error explaining that the file may be missing on the server or in an unsupported format. Contact your administrator if the problem persists.

## Download a recording

Click the **Download** button on a row to download the file.

The download opens in a new browser tab. Your browser can resume an interrupted download, and you can seek while streaming because the server supports range requests.

## Know the current limits

You can filter by camera only. There is no date filter yet.

There is no event-anchored playback view yet. To connect an event with its video, note the event time in **Events** and find the matching recording here by camera and start time.

There is no MKV remux download. Downloads use the stored file format.
