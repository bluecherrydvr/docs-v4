title: "Live view"
description: "Watch your cameras live in the camera grid."
---

Open **Live View** in the sidebar to watch your cameras.

The page shows a grid of camera tiles. Streams start automatically when the page loads. Your grid is kept for the rest of the browser session.

![The live view grid with the layout toolbar above it](/images/live-grid.png)

## Read the camera tiles

Each tile shows one camera. Hover over a tile to see its name and status.

A green **LIVE** badge means the stream is playing. A red **OFFLINE** badge means the camera is not streaming.

If you see **No cameras available**, no cameras are assigned to your account. Ask your administrator to check your camera permissions.

While the page loads, you see **Loading cameras...**.

## Control a PTZ camera

If a camera supports pan-tilt-zoom, hovering over its tile shows PTZ controls at the bottom of the tile.

Press and hold an arrow button to pan or tilt the camera. Release the button to stop the movement.

Use the **+** and **−** buttons to zoom in and out. Use the stop button to halt the camera immediately.

To return to a saved position, pick it from the **Preset** list and click **Go**. To save the current position, type a name in the **Save position as...** field and click **Save**. To remove the selected preset, click the delete button.

Cameras without PTZ show no controls.

## Arrange the grid

Click a preset (**1**, **4**, **9**, **16**) for a square grid, or use **+R** / **−R** and **+C** / **−C** to add and remove rows and columns. Shrinking the grid drops tiles that fall outside it.

Click an empty tile to assign a camera to it. Hover over a filled tile to assign a different camera or empty the tile.

![Picking a camera for an empty tile](/images/live-camera-picker.png)

## Save named layouts

Use the layout dropdown to load one of your saved layouts.

![Loading a saved layout from the dropdown](/images/live-layouts-menu.png)

Click **Save** to overwrite the current layout with the grid on screen, or **Save as…** to store it under a new name.

![Saving the current grid under a new name](/images/live-save-layout.png)

Click **Delete** to remove the current layout, or **Clear** to empty the grid.

Layouts are per user, and layouts you save in the classic web UI appear here too.

## Know the current limits

Streams play without sound. Audio support is planned but not available yet.

Active-camera polling is not available.
