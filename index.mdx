Bluecherry DVR v4 is the web interface for your Bluecherry surveillance server.

You use it to watch live cameras and find recordings. You also manage cameras, users, and notifications in the same place.

It is built for DVR owners and operators. You need no developer skills to use it.

After you log in, you land on **Live View**. You reach every other section from the sidebar.

<Tip>
  New here? Follow the [5-minute tour](/quickstart) to check your cameras and find your first recording.
</Tip>

## What you can do

<Card title="Watch live cameras" icon="video" href="/live">
  Check every camera at a glance on the **Live View** grid.
</Card>

<Card title="Find recordings" icon="film" href="/recordings">
  Filter the **Recordings** list by camera, then play or download a clip.
</Card>

<Card title="Review events" icon="zap" href="/events">
  Open the **Event Log**, inspect a motion or system event, and add a comment.
</Card>

<Card title="Manage cameras" icon="camera" href="/devices">
  Add cameras on the **Device Management** page and tune schedules and motion detection.
</Card>

<Card title="Manage users" icon="users" href="/users">
  Create operator accounts and control who can see each camera.
</Card>

<Card title="Set up notifications" icon="bell" href="/notifications">
  Get email alerts for motion and manage webhook integrations.
</Card>

<Card title="Maintain the system" icon="hard-drive" href="/system">
  Change general settings, check storage, back up the database, read logs, and view server statistics.
</Card>
