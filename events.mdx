---
title: Events
description: Browse the event log, filter by camera, inspect event details, and add comments.
---

Open **Events** in the sidebar to review what your cameras recorded.

The **Event Log** page lists events newest first. Each row shows **Date & Time**, **Device**, **Type**, and **Info**.

## Filter by camera

Use the **Filter by Camera** dropdown to show one camera. Select **All Cameras** to show every camera again.

## Move between pages

Each page holds 50 events. Click **Previous** or **Next** to move between pages. The counter shows **Page** X of Y.

## Inspect an event

Click the eye icon in **Actions** to open an event. The dialog shows the event type, the camera name, and the timestamp.

Tags appear as badges at the top of the dialog. If the event has no tags, you see **No tags**.

## Read and add comments

The **Comments** section lists every comment with its author and timestamp. If nobody commented yet, you see **No comments yet.**

Type into the **Add a comment...** field and click **Add** to save. You can also press Enter to save. Comments hold up to 160 characters.
