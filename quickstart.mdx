This guide takes you from the login screen to your first recording in about five minutes.

## What you need

You need the web address of your Bluecherry server. Ask the person who installed it if you do not have it.

You also need a username and password. Your administrator creates them for you.

## Log in

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the web UI">
    Open your server address in a browser. You see the **Bluecherry DVR** login card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your credentials">
    Type your username into **Username or Email**. Type your password into **Password**. Then select **Sign in**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You land on **Live View**. The sidebar on the left lists every section.

<Tip>
  If **Sign in** shows an error, check your password and try again. Ask your administrator to reset your account if you stay locked out.
</Tip>

## Take the 5-minute tour

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the live view">
    Look at the camera grid on **Live View**. Each tile shows one camera. If a camera is missing, open **Devices** and check that the camera exists.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find a recording">
    Open **Recordings**. Filter the list by camera if you have many. Select the play control on a row to watch it, or the download control to save it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Acknowledge an event">
    Open **Events**. Select the eye icon on an event row to open its details. Type a note into **Add a comment...** and select **Add**. Your note shows under **Comments** with your name and the time.
  </Step>
</Steps>
