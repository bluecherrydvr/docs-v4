## Unreleased / v4.0 preview

The v4 web interface replaces the legacy web UI. You log in at `/login` and manage your DVR from a new dashboard.

### Devices

You manage cameras on the **Device Management** page (`/devices`).

- You add a camera with **Add Camera** and edit or delete cameras from the list.
- You only see cameras you are allowed to access.
- You tune motion detection per camera with a motion-map editor.
- You set recording schedules per camera or for the whole system with **Global schedule**.
- You configure PTZ settings and presets, manage drivers with **PTZ drivers**, and move PTZ cameras live.

### Live

You watch your cameras on the **Live View** page (`/live`).

- You see a live grid of your cameras in the browser.

### Recordings

You find and play recordings on the **Recordings** page (`/media`).

- You filter the recordings list to find what you need.
- You play a recording in a dialog and download it with resumable downloads.

### Events

You review motion and system events on the **Event Log** page (`/events`).

- You filter the event list and open any event for detail.
- You add comments to events and view their tags.

### Notifications

You set up alerts on the **Notifications** page (`/notifications`).

- You create email rules with schedules in the **Email rules** card.
- You test a rule with a test send before you rely on it.
- You configure your mail server in the **Mail server** card and send a test email.
- You create webhooks in the **Webhooks** card and test-fire them.

### Users

You manage accounts on the **User Management** page (`/users`).

- You create users with **Add User** and edit or delete them.
- You grant access flags such as admin setup access per user.

### System

You administer storage, backup, logs, and statistics on the **System** page (`/system`).

- You add storage folders with **Add location** and check each path with **Check**.
- You see disk usage, free space, and recording-time estimates per location.
- You save all locations at once with **Save storage locations**.
- You prepare a database backup with **Prepare backup** and fetch it with **Download**.
- You restore from a backup archive (`.tgz`) and confirm with **Confirm restore**.
- You watch host CPU, memory, uptime, and `bc-server` status in the **Server status** card.
- You read server and web logs in the **Logs** card and grab a full bundle with **Download all**.
- You count events grouped by year, month, day, or hour in the **Event statistics** card with **Run**.
